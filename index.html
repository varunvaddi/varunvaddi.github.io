Travel and hospitality

Urban Mobility Analytics Platform

What would it take to forecast demand and price taxis in real time? I used NYC taxi data (8.6M+ trips and $180M+ in quarterly revenue) to find out.

What I built
A pipeline that mixes batch and streaming. Snowflake, dbt and Dockerized Airflow handle the heavy lifting with 37 automated validations, and Kafka brings in live demand signals at 10 messages a second.
What changed
Feature prep went from 6 hours to 23 seconds, a 95% cut, at 99.88% data quality. Data now arrives in under 5 minutes instead of in 24-hour batches.
What I learned from the data
Demand jumps 30% at peak hours, credit card trips bring in an 18% revenue premium, and a handful of high-value corridors should shape how drivers are allocated and priced.

Snowflake, dbt, Airflow, Kafka, Docker, Tableau

Media and tech

Digital Ads Risk Engine

Can an AI check an ad against Google's policies and show its work? I built a retrieval system over 316 policy chunks that explains every decision with citations.

What I built
A 6-node LangGraph state machine: classify the query, retrieve with BM25, BGE-large and cross-encoder reranking, retry when quality is low, generate with Ollama llama3.2, validate against the policy path, and escalate when confidence is low.
What changed
Decision accuracy went from 80% in v1 to 90%, with 77.8% Recall@5 and 0.778 MRR. Confidence calibration jumped from 29% to 81.4%.
How I checked it
An 8-metric evaluation suite using a dataset generated through Ollama: Recall@5, MRR, Precision@5, P95 latency, and LLM-judged faithfulness (0.63), context recall (0.80) and context precision (0.80).

RAG, LangGraph, Ollama, FAISS, BGE-large, BM25, LangChain, Pydantic, Streamlit, Python

Retail

Grocery ML and Recommendation Platform

Which customers will reorder, which are about to leave, and what is each one worth? I worked through 10.6M grocery orders to answer all three.

What I built
Five models: reorder prediction (80% AUC), customer lifetime value (90% R²), churn detection (77% AUC), RFM segmentation into 7 groups, and 149 basket rules with lifts up to 296x. Everything runs on Databricks and Spark with MLflow tracking.
A mistake worth sharing
My first churn model scored 100% AUC, which was a red flag, not a win. It was data leakage. Fixing it brought the score down to an honest 77%, and Hyperopt tuning then added 2%. I used SHAP to explain what the models were doing.
What it adds up to
About $4.65M a year in opportunity: $3.45M from RFM strategies, $1.15M from basket bundles and $978K from churn prevention. The Streamlit dashboard includes a what-if simulator and an ROI calculator.

Databricks, XGBoost, MLflow, Hyperopt, Azure, Apache Spark, SHAP, Streamlit

Media and entertainment

Movie Data Analytics Platform

How far can you get predicting a movie's rating from public data? I built the whole stack on AWS to find out, starting from 12.3M raw IMDb records.

What I built
An AWS Glue PySpark pipeline that boils 12.3M records down to 309K clean movies at 99%+ data quality. Decade-partitioned Parquet is 10x smaller than CSV and costs nothing to query in Athena.
What changed
43 engineered features over 4 XGBoost iterations moved R² from 0.306 to 0.664. The SageMaker endpoint predicts Schindler's List at 9.11 against an actual 9.0.
Keeping it running
EventBridge and Lambda trigger three Glue jobs every day, about 16 minutes end to end with no manual steps. Grafana and SNS send alerts if anything fails.

AWS Glue, SageMaker, Athena, XGBoost, EventBridge, Lambda, Grafana, PySpark
